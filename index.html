The Original Proposal

I want to explore collective emotional cartography.

Context: Reimagining Rider Speak

The idea came from doing homework for a different class, linked above. In short, in this class, I would like to make a map interface in which:

  • A user can walk around the city, choose a meaningful place, and record an audio snippet of an emotional narrative. Ideas of topics could be where they met their first friend, or finally decided to quit a job, or lost someone important to them.
  • Other users can explore the city by filtering the map based on an emotion. They would then have to physically go to the markers illustrated on the map to listen to other users’ audio recordings.

This interests me because:

  • I was born in New York, but I moved away when I was only 2 years old. Part of me coming here was because I wanted to learn what the place I was born in is like. But I don't want to merely explore the city through the lens of a tourist.
  • So I feel like this project can help navigate and learn about a city at a much deeper level and highlight invisible landmarks. For instance, maybe there's a particular tree where a lot of people reflect and come to life-changing epiphanies.
  • What would be even cooler is if we could get everyone in our cohort and maybe even onwards to record something in every city we go to. It would be a nice community-building exercise.
    • This makes me think that I could also create a way to let users record in groups or communities and filter the map accordingly.
